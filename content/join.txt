Join The CSE Cheese Society

Membership is free, meetings are monthly, and the cheese is real.
Fill out the form and the Keeper of the Cave will be in touch.

- Name (text, required)
- Email (email, required)
- Which family calls to you? (select: Fresh, Soft-ripened, Blue,

Why join?

- Monthly tastings. The Society buys the cheese. You bring opinions.
- Voting rights in the Cheese of the Month election.
- Access to THE CAVE (a mini-fridge, but a very serious one).
- A laminated membership card featuring the cheese of your choice.
- Amnesty program for recovering blue-cheese skeptics.
